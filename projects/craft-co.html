Menorca

Menorca needed a storefront that felt as premium as the product — a high-end ecommerce experience that increases perceived value instead of looking like a generic Shopify theme.

Live site ↗
Client
Menorca
Year
2025
Category
SMB / Local Business
Location
Portland, United States

The Challenge

Menorca wanted to launch a Shopify storefront without the site itself signaling "template" — the previous approach relied on standard product-grid layouts that undersold the brand's summer, editorial aesthetic.

The Solution

Built on Shopify with Framer Commerce, the new site pairs full-bleed lifestyle photography with tightly curated collections — Most Wanted, Essentials, Summer 26 — so browsing feels like flipping through a lookbook rather than scanning a catalog. Intuitive navigation keeps the seamless checkout experience front and center.
