TaxFirm

TaxFirm needed a site that read less like a generic accounting brochure and more like a strategic partner — the kind of firm that handles complex, worldwide tax situations.

The Challenge

Clients researching tax advisors were choosing based on trust signals they couldn't find on TaxFirm's old site — no team faces, no real client outcomes, just a list of services.

The Solution

The rebuilt site puts the team — Sophia Ramirez, Jonathan Lee, Amanda Patel — and hard numbers (1,452+ happy clients, 10+ years experience) up front, and swaps generic service descriptions for real case studies: a freelance writer stabilizing irregular income, a couple planning a home purchase without financial stress. Visitors can see themselves in the outcome.
