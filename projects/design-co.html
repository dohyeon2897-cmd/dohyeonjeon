Design & Co

A premium, minimal Framer template crafted for interior design studios and creative professionals. Design & Co focuses on strong visual presentation, refined typography, and a structured layout system that highlights projects with clarity and sophistication.

The Challenge

The objective was to create an interior design template that feels high-end without relying on heavy visuals or unnecessary complexity. Many design templates in this space feel cluttered or overly stylized. The challenge was to develop a clean, gallery-focused layout that allows imagery to lead while maintaining strong hierarchy and usability.

The Solution

Design & Co was built around a structured grid system with generous spacing and elegant typography. Components were designed for flexibility, allowing users to easily update layouts, showcase projects, and adapt branding. Subtle interactions enhance the browsing experience while keeping the overall aesthetic calm, modern, and premium.
