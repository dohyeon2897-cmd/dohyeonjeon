Claritax

Claritax needed a site that could speak to six very different industries — from SaaS startups to construction firms — without diluting its core promise of clear, compliant tax strategy.

Live site ↗
Client

The Challenge

Prospective clients across such different industries have different fears about taxes, and a one-size-fits-all pitch wasn't converting. Claritax's old site listed services but gave no sense of industry-specific expertise or measurable results.

The Solution

The site was restructured around six dedicated industry tracks — Technology & SaaS, E-Commerce, Professional Services, Healthcare, Construction, and Real Estate — each with its own compliance concerns spelled out. A four-step process and hard numbers ($25M+ in identified tax savings, 98% client retention) replace vague reassurance with evidence.
