Greenora

A modern and refreshing Framer template designed for gardening and landscaping businesses. Greenora combines clean structure with natural aesthetics to showcase services, projects, and client testimonials in a clear and approachable way.

The Challenge

The goal was to create a landscaping template that felt modern without losing its organic warmth. Many industry websites rely on outdated layouts or overly decorative visuals. The challenge was to balance natural design elements with a structured, scalable system that works for small businesses and growing studios alike.

The Solution

Greenora was built with a flexible component system and responsive layouts to ensure easy customization. A clean grid structure, soft color palette, and spacious typography create a calm and trustworthy presence. The template includes reusable sections and CMS-ready layouts, allowing users to adapt the design effortlessly to their brand and content.
