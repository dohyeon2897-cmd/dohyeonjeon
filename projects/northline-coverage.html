Midlands

Midlands needed a landing page focused entirely on one action — getting a personalized quote — for a campaign promoting their bundled home, car, and travel coverage.

Live site ↗
Client
Midlands
Year
2025
Category
Insurance / Landing Page
Location
Minneapolis, United States

The Challenge

Insurance shoppers comparing providers rarely read past the first screen, so Midlands' campaign traffic needed an immediate, unambiguous reason to request a quote instead of bouncing to a competitor.

The Solution

The page opens directly with the bundle offer — 10% off when insuring more than one thing — backed by real claims-experience testimonials instead of generic trust badges. A single "Get quote" path stays visible throughout, with coverage types (Home, Car, Travel, Health, Life, Pet) laid out for quick self-selection.
