Studio Form

A conceptual product showcase built around AI-generated studio photography of a modern sofa collection. Studio Form explores how clean layouts and controlled lighting can elevate product presentation in a refined, gallery-style digital experience.

Client
Personal Concept Project
Year
2025
Category
Art Direction / Design System
Location
Concept Project

The Challenge

The objective was to transform a series of studio-shot sofa visuals into a cohesive digital experience. Without an existing brand identity or client direction, the challenge was to create a structured layout that felt premium and intentional while allowing the product imagery to remain the focal point.

The design needed to feel minimal, modern, and product-driven — without becoming visually empty or overly decorative.

The Solution

The solution centered on strong visual hierarchy and generous spacing to create a gallery-like presentation. A neutral color palette and refined typography were used to complement the studio lighting and textures of the furniture.

Subtle transitions and structured sections guide users through the collection while maintaining focus on the product itself. The final result is a clean, high-end concept that demonstrates how minimal design can elevate product storytelling.
