Beautyco

Beautyco is a hair & beauty training school that needed its site to do more than look polished — it had to convert curious visitors into enrolled students. The site pairs course discovery with credibility signals to move people from browsing to booking.

The Challenge

Beauty training is a crowded, trust-sensitive category — prospective students weigh accreditation, instructor quality, and real outcomes before committing to a course. Beautyco's old site described its programs well but gave visitors no reason to believe the results, and enrollment inquiries stalled before a call ever happened.

The Solution

The site was rebuilt around proof: certified-tutor credentials, a real-salon training environment, and before-and-after student work displayed prominently rather than buried in a portfolio page. Course cards lead with outcomes, video testimonials from graduates reinforce credibility, and an "Enroll Now" path stays visible from the hero through every course page.
