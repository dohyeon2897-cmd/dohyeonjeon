Velvera

Velvera needed a landing page that did more than look good — it had to turn hair-concern research into booked appointments, with clear service pricing and real client transformations up front.

Live site ↗
Client
Velvera
Year
2026
Category
Beauty / Booking Platform
Location
Amsterdam, Netherlands

The Challenge

Hair salon shoppers often bounce between review sites and Instagram before ever visiting a salon's own site, so Velvera's page had to earn trust and drive a booking in a single visit. The previous layout listed services without pricing or proof, leaving visitors unsure of cost or fit before reaching out.

The Solution

The page opens with a problem-to-solution structure — common hair concerns paired directly with the treatments that fix them — then moves into transparent, priced service cards and a stylist team section with real names and specialties. A single appointment form captures preferred service and date up front, so every visit ends in either a booking or a clear next step.
