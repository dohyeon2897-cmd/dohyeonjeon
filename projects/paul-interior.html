Paul Interior

Paul Interior is a boutique interior design studio website designed to showcase refined spaces and curated aesthetics. The site balances imagery and text with clean layouts, highlighting the studio's expertise in modern yet timeless interior solutions.

Live site ↗
Client
Paul Interior Studio
Year
2025
Category
Web Design / Art Direction
Location
London, United Kingdom

The Challenge

Paul Interior needed a digital presence that reflected the elegance and precision of their physical spaces. Their previous website lacked visual hierarchy and did not effectively showcase their portfolio. The challenge was to create a refined, minimal layout that allowed their work to stand out while maintaining a strong brand identity.

The Solution

The solution focused on clarity and structure. A grid-based layout was implemented to highlight projects with generous spacing and strong typography. Subtle interactions were added to enhance the browsing experience without distracting from the visuals. The result is a sophisticated, content-driven website that feels calm, modern, and aligned with the studio's aesthetic values.
